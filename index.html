03 / Behind the creativity

Hi, I'm Temitope.

“I notice the little moments, then find the story worth telling.”

I'm a Lagos-based content creator and storyteller who loves turning everyday observations into relatable, engaging work. I create across formats — from scripts and short-form video to carousels and brand storytelling.

Sometimes I develop the idea from scratch. Other times, I take a brief or script and bring it to life. Either way, I care about the detail that makes people stop, connect and remember.
